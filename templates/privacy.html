Privacy

What Rootly stores

  • Your username, email address and, if you choose one, your region.
  • Your password, stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read.
  • The plants you add, and their nicknames, locations, notes, care log, reminders and health records.
  • Photos you upload of your plants.

What Rootly does with it

It is used only to show you your own plants and care history. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you. There are no adverts and no analytics trackers.

Other services

When you search for a plant, the words you search for are sent to the Trefle plant database to find matches. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Reminders on your phone

The mobile app's reminder notifications are created on your own phone. They are not sent through a notification service.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, at any time: in the mobile app under Account, or on this website on the Account page. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
